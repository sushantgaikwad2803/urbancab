Frequently Asked Questions

Get quick answers regarding ordering, pricing, site delivery, foundation needs, and custom fabrication options.

Pricing & Payments

How is the price of a portable cabin or container calculated?

Pricing depends on four primary variables:

  • Dimensions: Overall length, width, and height (e.g., 10'×10', 20'×10', 40'×8').
  • Material & Insulation: Insulation type (PUF, EPS, Glasswool) and panel thickness.
  • Interior Fit-Outs: Flooring type, number of windows, electrical fittings, partition walls, and attached toilets.
  • Delivery Distance: Transport distance from our Kalyan Phata, Thane manufacturing yard to your site.

Do you provide direct factory pricing?

Yes. Because we design, frame, and assemble all units directly at our Thane facility, you buy directly from the manufacturer with no middleman markups.

Site Delivery & Logistics

How are the cabins delivered to my project site?

Units are transported via flatbed or hydraulic trailers depending on unit size. For smaller units (10'×10' or 20'×10'), standard flatbed trucks are used. Larger or multi-module units are delivered sequentially.

Who handles offloading and crane unloading at the site?

All our cabins and modified containers are equipped with reinforced top/bottom corner lifting lugs or crane hooks for easy offloading. Offloading cranes/forklifts at the destination site are typically arranged by the buyer, though our team can coordinate local crane services upon request.

How long does manufacturing and delivery take?

  • Standard Units (e.g., 20'×10' Site Offices): Ready in 5 to 8 days.
  • Custom / Multi-Story Installations: Ready in 10 to 15 days depending on specs.
  • In-Stock Units: Available for same-day or next-day dispatch from our Kalyan Phata yard.

Foundation & Site Preparation

Do portable cabins require a concrete foundation?

No heavy concrete foundation is required. You only need a flat, leveled surface.

What ground preparation is recommended before delivery?

To prevent moisture accumulation and ensure long-term structural leveling, we recommend placing the cabin on:

  • Level concrete blocks or paver blocks at key support corners.
  • A compacted gravel/hardcore bed.
  • A plain cement concrete (PCC) pad (optional for long-term setups).

Customization & Build Options

Can I customize the interior layout and dimensions?

Yes. Every cabin can be custom-built to your exact floor plan, including custom partition walls, executive desks, pantry spaces, attached toilets, and air conditioning cutouts.

What insulation and weatherproofing options do you offer?

  • Wall Panels: Pre-painted galvanized iron (GI) sheets with high-density PUF or EPS sandwich cores for heat reduction.
  • Ceilings: Insulated false ceilings with integrated LED panels.
  • Roofing: Sloped, leak-proof corrugated steel sheet overlays designed for heavy monsoon endurance.

Can cabins be stacked to form two-story (G+1) structures?

Yes. We engineer heavy-gauge steel corner posts and structural framing specifically designed for vertical stacking (G+1) with external steel staircases and walkways.
