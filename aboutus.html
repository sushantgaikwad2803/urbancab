Built Strong. Engineered Smart. Delivered Anywhere.

Thane's Premier Manufacturer of Heavy-Duty Portable Cabins & Custom Container Solutions.

At Urban Cab Portable Cabins, we transform industrial space requirements into rugged, comfortable, and fully functional modular environments. Based in Kalyan Phata, Thane, we specialize in end-to-end design, fabrication, and supply of heavy-duty portable cabins, site offices, bunkhouses, and customized shipping containers.

Whether you need a quick site setup for a multi-million dollar construction project, an executive site office with full amenities, or specialized storage containers, our structures are engineered to perform under demanding Indian weather conditions—delivering long-term durability without compromising on safety or interior comfort.

Our Manufacturing Standards & Materials

Every component is chosen for durability, safety, and performance.

Structural Skeleton 100mm × 50mm 'I' Beam frame with cross members of 50mm M.S. Pipe.
Panels & Insulation GI Sheet panels (Essex/Uttam Gava/Posco) with 8mm MDF board and glass wool insulation (16kg/m³).
Flooring System 18mm Cement Fiber Board overlaid with anti-skid PVC vinyl carpet for maximum load capacity.
Finish & Paint Phosphating + 1 coat epoxy primer + 2 coats epoxy paint for superior corrosion resistance.
Doors & Windows Godrej door closure with L-Drop locking + powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with safety grills.
Electrical Systems Concealed copper wiring (Polycab/Havells) with LED lights, fans, switches, sockets, AC point, and MCB.
